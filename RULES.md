# Rulebook (full, spoilers)

This is every page of the troll's book. In a real duel each player only sees some of it. Reading this first spoils the game.

| # | Title | Visibility at start | Rule |
|---|-------|---------------------|------|
| 1 | The Troll | Public | Every side has one Troll (the King). Take the enemy Troll and its owner must crown any other piece they have left as the new Troll. If a Troll falls and its owner has nothing left to crown, that player loses the duel. |
| 2 | Turns & Time | Public | Players alternate, one move per turn. Each player has ten minutes in total. After 120 moves the duel ends and the larger army wins (Pawn 1, Knight 3, Bishop 3, Rook 5, Queen 9). A tie is a draw. A player with no legal move loses. |
| 3 | Pawn Shuffle | Hidden | Pawns step one square forward or one square sideways, never backward and never diagonally. A Pawn captures only by stepping forward onto an enemy. |
| 4 | Ascension | Hidden | A Pawn that reaches the far edge of the bridge ascends and becomes a Rook, not a Queen. |
| 5 | Knight's Leap | Hidden | Knights move in an L and are the only pieces that may jump over others. Everything else is blocked by whatever stands in its path. |
| 6 | Bishop's Stride | Hidden | Bishops slide any distance diagonally, or step one square straight in any of the four directions. |
| 7 | Rook's Reach | Hidden | Rooks slide in straight lines, but never more than four squares in a single move. |
| 8 | Queen's Mercy | Hidden | The Queen slides any distance in a straight or diagonal line, but she refuses to capture Pawns. |
| 9 | Troll's Step | Hidden | A Troll (King) moves one square in any direction, diagonals included. |
| 10 | Royal Swap | Hidden | Instead of moving, a Troll may swap places with a friendly Rook standing next to him. |
| 11 | Slippery Ice | Hidden | Hidden patches of ice lie across the middle of the bridge. A piece that lands on ice slides one extra square in the direction it was travelling, if that square is empty. Ice is only visible once someone has triggered it. |
| 12 | No Encores | Hidden | You may never move the same piece two turns in a row (unless it is the only piece you have left). |
| 13 | Sanctuary | Hidden | A piece standing in one of the four corners of the bridge cannot be captured. Trolls enjoy no such protection. |
| 14 | Old Grudge | Hidden | Knights may not capture Bishops. |

Setup is a standard chess layout on an 8x8 bridge: Cream (light) at the bottom, Moss (dark) at the top, Cream moves first. There is no check or checkmate. Both Kings are **Trolls** and are simply captured.

## How hidden rules work

- Both players know pages 1 and 2 from the start.
- Each player also begins with 3 private pages, chosen at random and never the same for both players.
- **Fumble:** an illegal move is refused, the page you broke is opened for you, and you lose 20 seconds.
- **Loose pages:** after every 4th move you make, one random unknown page slides out for you.
- **Shared reveals:** page 11 (ice) opens for both when someone lands on ice; page 4 opens for both when a pawn ascends.
- **Caught cheats:** the page that was broken opens for both players.
- When the duel ends, the whole book opens for both players.
- The board never highlights legal moves, because that would leak the rules.

## The cheating system

- Each player starts with **1 cheat token**.
- On your turn, press **Cheat** to arm it, then make any move you like with one of your pieces, as long as:
  - the destination is within 3 squares (any direction),
  - it is not occupied by your own piece,
  - it does not capture the enemy Troll.
- If the move turns out to be legal anyway, it simply plays and you keep the token. Otherwise the token is spent, the page you broke opens for you, and the move stands. Your opponent is told nothing.
- Every cheat breaks one specific rule page (the piece's movement rule, or a restriction such as No Encores, Sanctuary, Old Grudge or Queen's Mercy).

## Accusations

- Open the **ACCUSE** tab on the side of the book (or press Space), then press ACCUSE to enter accuse mode.
- Only on your own turn, before you move, and only against the opponent's most recent move. One accusation per move.
- Click the enemy piece you suspect, then name a rule page. You may name a page you have not uncovered.
- **Correct** (it was a cheat, you picked the right piece, and the right page): their move is undone, they lose their turn, you gain a cheat token, and the page opens for both of you.
- **Wrong:** you lose a cheat token, or 20 seconds if you have none. Nobody learns whether the move was a cheat.

## Trolls: losing your King

When a Troll is captured its owner does not lose straight away. They must **crown any other piece they have left** (click it, any type) and that piece becomes the new Troll (it moves like a Troll from then on). Play continues with the crowned side to move. Nobody can accuse or move until the crown is placed.

If a Troll falls and its owner has no other piece to crown, that player loses the duel.

## Winning

Take the last Troll of the enemy army, win on time (10 minutes each), or hold the bigger army after 120 moves. A player with no legal move loses.
